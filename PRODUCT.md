# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

People in Cartagena looking for a contemporary, premium but approachable salon experience on mobile or desktop.

## Product Purpose

Present Baskuñana Peluqueros' services and work, build confidence through transformations and reviews, and convert visits into appointment requests.

## Positioning

Precise technique and honest advice that enhance the client's own style.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: request an appointment.
- Secondary CTA: explore services.
- Proof: portfolio, before/after comparison, senior stylist badge, and demo testimonials.

## Brand Personality

Considered, confident, and warm.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic beige editorial templates, ornamental luxury clichés, excessive glass effects, and aggressive salon imagery.

## Design Principles

- Let hair and craft remain the visual focus.
- Use motion as proof of care, not decoration.
- Make booking effortless on a phone.
- Be explicit when content or functionality is demonstrative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with keyboard access, reduced-motion behavior, visible focus, semantic landmarks, and touch targets of at least 44px.
